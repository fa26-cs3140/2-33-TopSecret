Access Control

Authorization is based on the role in the authenticated Session.


ROLES

  STANDARD   list and read content
  ADMIN      standard capabilities plus content creation, account creation,
             audit review, and role promotion/demotion

The initial account is ADMIN. Later accounts begin as STANDARD. An administrator
cannot change their own role, which prevents the active administrator from
removing their own access.


ROLE OPERATIONS

  auth.promote(actingUsername, targetUsername)
  auth.demote(actingUsername, targetUsername)

The actor must be an administrator, the target must exist and the requested role
must differ from the target's current role.


SESSION AND ENFORCEMENT

AuthenticationService.sessionFor(username) creates a Session carrying the
username and role after login. AccessControl exposes capability checks for reading,
content creation, account creation, audit review and role management.

The interface uses these checks to decide which options to display, and the service
layer checks again before acting. A null session fails every capability check.


PERSISTENCE

The role is the third tab-separated field in each ciphered credential record.
Two-field records created before roles were introduced are read as STANDARD.
Unknown role values and records with more than three fields are rejected.

Tests cover role parsing, sessions, credential value semantics, every capability,
role changes through mocked and real credential stores, and menu visibility.
